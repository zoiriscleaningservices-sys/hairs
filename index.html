Frequently Asked Questions

How does the SMS booking confirmation work?

As soon as you submit the booking form, our Twilio cloud integration automatically sends an instant SMS text message directly to your cell phone number as well as to Cece's business line.

What is the difference between XS, Small, and Medium Knotless?

XS Knotless ($250) has micro square parts for ultimate density and longest longevity. Small Knotless ($200) features pencil-thick square parts with high fullness. Medium Knotless ($130) uses standard finger-width parting for a classic protective look.

How should I prepare for a Braid Down or Take Down?

For Braid Down ($20), please arrive with clean, washed and blown out hair. For Take Down ($40-$80), we gently unravel your braids, cleanse the scalp, and detangle your natural hair safely.
